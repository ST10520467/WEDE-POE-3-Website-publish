About Us at Whole Bakery

Whole Bakery History

A brief history of the small business, Whole Bakery, is that it was founded by Elijah Robinson in 2025. The reason he opened Whole Bakery is because he had a burning desire to do what he loved which is to bake and make bakery items, His family always complimented his bakery, so he decided to open it up to share and show people how good is bakery skills are.Whole Bakery is the Owners passion and this relfects on the pasteries.All of the recipies at whole Bakery are the owners.Whole Bakery has a unique history but it also has a bright future.

Whole Bakery Mission and vision

  • Mission:The Mission at Whole bakery is to bake and make bakery items daily; the items must be fresh and taste delicious for customers. They want to have good customer service.
  • Vision:The Vision at Whole Bakery is to become the favourite bakery amongst the community; they want to be known for their delicious itemsand making sure customers have an overall good experience.

Our team members at Whole Bakery

  • Sam who is the Pastry Chef: She is the person who bakes all the pastries

Our Core Values at Whole Bakery

We have 5 main core values

  • Our 1st core value is to serve high quaility,fresh and delicious items to our customers
  • Our 2nd core value is to keep a strict standard of baking everything fresh on a daily basis
  • Our 3rd core value is to have a warm welcome to every single customer we serve,we want out customers to get a homely feel.We want everyone to feel comfortable
  • Our 4th core value is to have and put passion and innovation into all of our daily baking
  • Our 5th core value is to make sure we have partnerships with local suppliers for the ingredients we need
